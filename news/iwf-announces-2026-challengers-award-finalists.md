## Source summary

IWF has announced 20 finalists for the 2026 Challengers Award from 84 entries by 65 exhibiting companies. Finalists will demonstrate live on August 25, 2026, with seven winners announced on August 26. The list includes entries from Felder Group USA (creator 1250, F1000 performance), SCM North America (Accord 500 Edge), Stiles Machinery (Venjakob Ven Air Vortex, ORRA Velocity Sorting Solution), and others. Additionally, 15 industry associations have been named IWF 2024 Industry Partners Alliance members, receiving benefits for the August 6-9, 2024 trade show.
