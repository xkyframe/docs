## Source summary

SATA has introduced the jet K, a pressure-fed spray gun designed for industrial and wood applications, featuring a labyrinth airflow system for smooth atomization, a taller spray pattern for increased coverage, and a lightweight design at 430 grams. It offers customizable trigger options and supports various nozzle sizes, including MaxLayer nozzles for high-build coatings, and can be used with multiple low-pressure delivery systems.

## Key facts

- SATA has introduced the jet K, a pressure-fed spray gun.
- The SATA jet K weighs 430 grams.
- The SATA jet K is 25% lighter than the SATAjet 3000 K.
- The SATA jet K features a labyrinth airflow system from the jet X.
- The SATA jet K has a taller spray pattern than the SATAjet 3000 K.
- The SATA jet K includes standard and Med-Large trigger sets.
- The SATA jet K is available with standard and MaxLayer nozzles.
- The SATA jet K is compatible with pressure tanks, diaphragm pumps, and plural component systems.
