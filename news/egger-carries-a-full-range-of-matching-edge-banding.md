## Source summary

EGGER Wood Products will showcase a wide array of edge banding options at IWF 2026, including matching, accent, and end grain options. The company states that these options help eliminate time spent sourcing materials, ensure a seamless consistent finish, and offer design flexibility for kitchens and closets. EGGER will be at booth C1622.
