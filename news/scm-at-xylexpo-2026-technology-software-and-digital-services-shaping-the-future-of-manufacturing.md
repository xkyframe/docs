# SCM at Xylexpo 2026: Technology, software and digital services shaping the future of manufacturing

## Source summary

At Xylexpo 2026, SCM showcased its vision for the factory of the future, emphasizing flexible automation, digital transformation, and comprehensive service solutions. The company reported 2025 revenues close to €900 million, with its Wood Division exceeding €500 million. Highlights included the accord 500 edge CNC machining centre with integrated edge banding, the IoT Solution platform connecting over 5,400 machines, and proactive remote support via Control Room. SCM invests 7% of revenues in R&D and holds 800 active patents.
