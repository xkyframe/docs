## Source summary

Oneida Air Systems will showcase its Smart Boost technology for high-pressure applications, such as CNCs and edge banders, at IWF 2026. The technology combines variable speed fan motors with cyclone separation, powered by U.S.-made brushless motors. It automatically adjusts fan speed to optimize CFM and static pressure, delivering up to twice the airflow of standard fixed speed collectors, making it suitable for long ducting runs and mixed sized tool ports. Systems are available in 3 to 5 horsepower, wall mount or freestanding, with a reinforced steel dust bin. Airlock and transfer blower packages are also available.
