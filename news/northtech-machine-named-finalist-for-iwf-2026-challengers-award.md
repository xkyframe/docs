## Source summary

Northtech Machine's iGlueSRCD, an automatic glue applicator for stile and rail cabinet door construction, is a finalist for the IWF 2026 Challengers Award. The machine automates glue application: operator places a part, runs a scan mode, loads two parts into one of two workstations, and with a button push applies exact glue amount. A touchscreen allows adjustment and program recall. Dual work zones enable continuous workflow. Northtech won the award in 2022 (iDovetail) and 2024 (iGlue). No company has won three consecutive shows; if selected, Northtech would make history.
