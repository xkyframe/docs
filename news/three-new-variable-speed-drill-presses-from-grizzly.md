# Three New Variable-Speed Drill Presses from Grizzly
> Grizzly Industrial has introduced three new variable-speed drill presses powered by variable-frequency drives: a 15-inch benchtop model, a 15-inch floor model, and a 17-inch floor model. The presses offer spindle speeds 
## Source summary

Grizzly Industrial has introduced three new variable-speed drill presses powered by variable-frequency drives: a 15-inch benchtop model, a 15-inch floor model, and a 17-inch floor model. The presses offer spindle speeds from 30 to 3000 RPM for precise control in drilling metal, wood, and general applications. Features include laser line guides, LED work lights, digital readouts, spindle depth stops, and cast-iron construction. The announcement was made by Jon Lamza, Director of Special Projects at Grizzly Industrial.
