## Source summary

MATEC 2026, the first edition of a new integrated trade fair in Milan, closed successfully. It combined Plast (plastics and rubber machinery), Xylexpo (woodworking and furniture technology), and Composites Future (composite materials) under one roof at Fiera Milano Rho. The event highlighted strong industry engagement, technological innovation, and cross-sector collaboration, with woodworking technology playing a central role. Organizers reported robust participation from exhibitors and professional visitors, signaling demand for advanced industrial solutions. The integrated format is expected to accelerate adoption of hybrid manufacturing systems.

## Key facts

- MATEC 2026 was the first edition of a new integrated trade fair held at Fiera Milano Rho in Milan.

- The event lasted four days.
- Composites Future made its debut at MATEC 2026.
