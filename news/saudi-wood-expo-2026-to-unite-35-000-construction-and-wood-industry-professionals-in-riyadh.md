## Source summary

Saudi Wood Expo 2026 will take place from August 30 to September 1, 2026, at the Riyadh Front Exhibition & Conference Center, co-located with Big 5 Construct Saudi. The event is expected to attract over 35,000 industry professionals, including suppliers, contractors, architects, and manufacturers. More than 200 exhibitors from over 30 countries will showcase 3,500+ products, including advanced woodworking machinery. The expo aims to support Saudi Arabia's construction growth under Vision 2030, with features like the Wood Design Trail and interactive demonstrations.
