## Source summary

Laguna Tools appointed Justin Cormier as Vice President of Operations. He will oversee manufacturing, supply chain, logistics, and inventory management, reporting to President and incoming CEO Matthew Thackray. Cormier previously led operations for the Americas region at Kongsberg Precision Cutting Systems/Multicam. The appointment aims to enhance operational efficiency, improve fulfillment performance, and support growth. Laguna Tools is a portfolio company of Hudson Ferry Capital.
