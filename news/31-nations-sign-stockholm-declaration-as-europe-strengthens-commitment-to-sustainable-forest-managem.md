## Source summary

31 European nations signed the Stockholm Declaration at Forest Europe's tenth Ministerial Conference, reaffirming commitment to sustainable forest management, cross-border cooperation on climate change, biodiversity, wildfires, workforce shortages, and wood construction.

The Stockholm Declaration ensures a long-term, stable supply of sustainably managed European wood—critical for our customers who depend on consistent hardwood and softwood for frame production. ## Key facts

- 31 European nations signed the Stockholm Declaration at Forest Europe's tenth Ministerial Conference.
- The conference was held in Stockholm, Sweden.
- Over 130 delegates from over 30 countries attended.
- Sweden's Minister Peter Kullgren chaired the event.
- Albania will chair Forest Europe from 1 January 2027.
- Forest Europe was established in 1990.
- Forest Europe holds ministerial conferences every 3 to 5 years.
- Forest Europe has 46 member countries.
