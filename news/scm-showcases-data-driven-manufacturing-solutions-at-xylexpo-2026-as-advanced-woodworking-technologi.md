# SCM showcases data-driven manufacturing solutions at Xylexpo 2026 as advanced woodworking technologies take centre stage

## Source summary

SCM is showcasing data-driven manufacturing solutions at Xylexpo 2026 in Milan, including the new accord 500 edge CNC machining centre with an exclusive edgebanding system, WinMES MES/MOM platform, and IoT digital services. The event runs until 12 June and emphasizes connected production, data utilization, and smart factory innovations for woodworking and advanced manufacturing.

## Key facts

- Xylexpo 2026 runs until 12 June at Fieramilano, Milan.
- SCM exhibits in Hall 14 at Xylexpo 2026.
- SCM showcased the new accord 500 edge CNC machining centre with an exclusive edgebanding system.
- The WinMES platform was developed with Tecnest for real-time monitoring.
- SCM offers IoT Solution digital services for data-driven insights.
- Demonstrations at the SCM booth cover edgebanding, sanding, finishing, windows/doors, flooring, and timber construction.
- Xylexpo is a biennial woodworking technology trade show in Milan.
