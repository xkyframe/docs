## Source summary

Blue Elephant CNC has launched a new furniture CNC solution to expand its woodworking production line. The machine is designed for panel furniture, cabinet making, and custom woodworking. Production is underway at the company's Jinan, China facility. Blue Elephant CNC supplies equipment to businesses in over 180 countries. The new model integrates with edge banders, drilling machines, and laser cuters for smart factory configurations.
