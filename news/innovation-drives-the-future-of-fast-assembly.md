# Innovation Drives the Future of Fast Assembly

## Source summary

Lockdowel has developed a patented fastening system that requires no glue, screws, or special tools. Customers report up to 60% faster assembly. The system uses keyhole slots machined on CNC machines for sturdy joints. It minimizes training and labor costs and enables flat-pack shipping. Products include fasteners, clips, spring pins, and drawer slides. Lockdowel will exhibit at IWF Booth BC707.
