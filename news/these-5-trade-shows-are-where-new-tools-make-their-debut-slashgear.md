## Source summary

The article lists five trade shows where new tools debut: International Woodworking Fair (IWF) in Atlanta (Aug 25-28, 2026), the largest North American woodworking show with over 26,500 attendees and 1,000+ exhibitors; Equip (Oct 20-23, 2026) for outdoor power equipment; World of Concrete (Jan 18-21, 2027) for construction; CONEXPO-CON/AGG (March 2029) for general construction; and Lowe's Creator Summit for home improvement. IWF is expanding with IWF West in San Antonio in 2027.

## Key facts

- International Woodworking Fair (IWF) in Atlanta is scheduled for August 25-28, 2026.
- IWF is the largest North American woodworking show with over 26,500 attendees and 1,000+ exhibitors.
- IWF West will launch in San Antonio, Texas in 2027.
- Equip 2026 is scheduled for October 20-23 at the Kentucky Exposition Center with nearly 30,000 attendees.
- World of Concrete 2027 is scheduled for January 18-21 at the Las Vegas Convention Center.
- CONEXPO-CON/AGG next occurs in March 2029, with over 140,000 attendees in 2026.
- IWF is the oldest and largest woodworking trade show in North America, held biennially.
