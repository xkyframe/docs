## Source summary

Salvador launched the Superpush 250 XL automatic pusher-type optimiser with chain loading at Xylexpo 2026. The machine combines straight cutting and drilling in a single process, handling sections up to 420 mm deep and 200 mm high. It features an integrated 5-spindle independent drilling unit for simultaneous operations. The event took place at Rho Milano Exhibition Centre, Hall 14 – Stand H11, and ended June 12, 2026. Salvador exports 90% of production to over 50 countries.
