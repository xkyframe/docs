# Prefinished panels come of age

## Source summary

The article discusses the resurgence of prefinished panels in woodworking, highlighting advances in waterborne and UV finishes that make factory-coated panels a viable option for cabinet interiors and wall paneling. Benefits include reduced labor costs, improved uniformity, faster job flow, elimination of sanding and drying time, and lower environmental impact. Several suppliers are listed, including States Industries, Brown Wood Inc., Hardwoods Specialty Products, L.L. Johnson Lumber, Uniboard, WalzCraft, and Frank Paxton Lumber, each offering various prefinished panel options with UV-cured, antimicrobial, or chemical-resistant coatings. The article also mentions new flexible tambour panels from Osborne Wood Products and decorative elements from Castlewood.

## Key facts

- Prefinished panels reduce spray booth time and labor costs.
- Factory coatings use robotic and AI-managed line equipment.
- UV coatings produce low or zero VOCs.
- States Industries offers NOVA SI antimicrobial finish with silver ions.
- Hardwoods Specialty Products supplies Shinnoki panels in 18 species.
- Uniboard's Eclipse line uses thermal-lacquering anti-fingerprint technology.
- WalzCraft offers UV-coated maple veneer-core plywood in 1/4", 1/2", and 3/4" thicknesses.
- Osborne Wood Products introduced flexible tambour panels in 5 species.
