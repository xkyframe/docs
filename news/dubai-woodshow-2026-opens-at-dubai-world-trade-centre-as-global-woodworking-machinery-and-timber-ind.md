## Source summary

Dubai WoodShow 2026 opened at Dubai World Trade Centre from June 22–24, 2026, showcasing advanced woodworking machinery, engineered timber, furniture production systems, and automated manufacturing. The event includes the Global WoodShow Conference focusing on hybrid materials, circular production, and climate-responsive design. Sustainability is a key theme, aligned with UAE Vision 2031, Dubai 2040 Urban Master Plan, and Net Zero 2050. Exhibitors report increased interest from MENA region developers and contractors. The show highlights automation, AI integration, and precision manufacturing as structural trends in the woodworking sector.

## Key facts

- Dubai WoodShow 2026 takes place from June 22 to June 24, 2026 at Dubai World Trade Centre.
- The event occupies Halls 6, 7, and 8.

- The Global WoodShow Conference covers hybrid materials, circular production, and climate-responsive design.
