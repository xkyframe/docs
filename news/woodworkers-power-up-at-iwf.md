## Source summary

IWF 2026, North America's largest woodworking event, will be held Aug. 25-28 at the Georgia World Congress Center in Atlanta. The theme is 'Power Up Your Business,' featuring new technology, machinery, materials, and supplies. Expected exhibitors: nearly 1,100; registered attendees: over 26,500. New features include a Mass Timber and Prefabricated Construction Products Showcase. Education programs cover business management, manufacturing, marketing, and workforce topics. The Challengers Award and Design Emphasis competitions return. Show hours vary by hall.
