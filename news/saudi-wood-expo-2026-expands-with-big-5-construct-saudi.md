## Source summary

The third edition of Saudi Wood Expo, co-located with Big 5 Construct Saudi, will take place from August 30 to September 1, 2026 at the Riyadh Front Exhibition & Conference Center. Organized by dmg events, the combined event aims to connect wood and construction sectors, offering exhibitors increased exposure to qualified buyers and networking opportunities within Saudi Arabia's growing construction market. The expo showcases wood products, woodworking machinery, furniture components, interior solutions, and sustainable building materials, with expanded exhibition space and enhanced facilities.
