## Source summary

Ennis Henry, sales specialist at MachMotion, presented at the Closets Conference & Expo in West Palm Beach, Florida on June 15, 2026. He discussed how custom closet and storage manufacturers can automate existing CNC machines without buying new machinery by retrofitting with components such as automatic tool changers and loading/unloading systems. This approach aims to increase throughput and minimize material handling, offering a cost-effective alternative to purchasing new equipment.

## XKY Frame Insight

Retrofitting existing CNC routers with automatic tool changers and loading systems is a smart way to boost throughput without a full machine purchase. For picture frame shops, this approach can reduce material handling and labor costs while keeping your current underpinner and miter saw workflow intact.
