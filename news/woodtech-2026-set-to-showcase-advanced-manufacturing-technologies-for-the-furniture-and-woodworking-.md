## Source summary

WoodTech 2026, the 39th edition of the woodworking technology exhibition, will be held at the Istanbul Tüyap Fair and Congress Centre from 22 to 25 October 2026. Organized by RX Tüyap in cooperation with the Woodworking Machinery Manufacturers Association (AİMSAD) and supported by the Turkish Ministry of Trade and the European Federation of Woodworking Machinery Manufacturers (EUMABOIS), the event will showcase CNC machinery, panel processing equipment, automation systems, cutting tools, surface treatment technologies, production software, and smart factory applications. The exhibition aims to help furniture and woodworking manufacturers improve productivity, accuracy, and flexibility. Financial support for exhibitors is available up to 1,292,800 Turkish Lira, covering up to 50% of participation costs, under the Domestic Sectoral Fairs programme as a 'Prestigious Fair'. The event attracts an international audience and serves as a business networking platform.
