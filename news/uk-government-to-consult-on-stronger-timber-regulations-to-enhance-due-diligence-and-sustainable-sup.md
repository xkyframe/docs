## Source summary

The UK Government announced a public consultation to strengthen timber regulations, requiring businesses in Great Britain with annual turnover over £1 million to conduct due diligence verifying that timber products comply with local legislation in the country of origin. The consultation, expected later this year, precedes the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) effective end of 2026. Timber Development UK (TDUK) supports stronger regulation but warns against directly copying the EUDR, advocating a balanced model that considers domestic market conditions and international trading relationships. The consultation also addresses Northern Ireland's regulatory position under post-Brexit arrangements.
