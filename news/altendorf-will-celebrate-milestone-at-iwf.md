## Source summary

Altendorf will celebrate its 120th anniversary at IWF 2026 in Atlanta, showcasing its full machine range at Booth B7972. The company emphasizes intuitive operation, short setup times, and maximum productivity. The F 35 sliding table saw is the centerpiece, along with the Hand Guard AI safety system, F 45 slider, and K 36 and F 5 edge banders. Altendorf America's Managing Director Darren McCarthy highlighted the strongest machine lineup ever brought to an American trade show.
