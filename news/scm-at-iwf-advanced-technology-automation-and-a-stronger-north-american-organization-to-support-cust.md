## Source summary

SCM will showcase a comprehensive range of woodworking technologies, automation solutions, and digital services at IWF 2026, with a nearly 25,000 sq ft booth. Highlights include the Lean Cell (entry-level high-speed nesting with integrated downstream operations and robotic handling) and the North American premiere of the Accord 500 Edge CNC machining center (finalist for the IWF 2026 Challengers Award). Digital solutions for programming, integration, and connectivity will be featured. New leadership: Marco Patriarchi as Regional Executive for North America and Tiago Costa as CEO USA. SCM will also participate in the Mass Timber & Pre-Fab Showcase.
