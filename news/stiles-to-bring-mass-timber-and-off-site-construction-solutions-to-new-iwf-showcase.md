## Source summary

Stiles Machinery will participate in the International Woodworking Fair's new Mass Timber & Pre-Fab Showcase, debuting at IWF 2026 (August 25-28, Atlanta). Partnering with HOMAG Group companies Kallesoe, System TM, and WEINMANN, Stiles will demonstrate connected solutions for manufacturing glulam beams, CLT panels, and prefabricated construction elements. The showcase includes a Kallesoe RF Press for high-speed gluing and a WEINMANN Wallteq multi-function bridge for automated wall panel sheathing. Casey Harless, Stiles' Director of Construction Element Solutions, noted that mass timber and off-site construction are fast-growing segments requiring the right technology partners.
