## Source summary

The article discusses the growing demand for compact outdoor woodworking products like balcony planters, raised beds, and potting benches in Europe, driven by urban living trends. Eurostat data shows 47.7% of the EU population lived in flats in 2023, and 73% in EU cities. RHS Chelsea Flower Show highlights balcony gardens, and Google trends show 'mini garden' at an all-time high. Material choices include Scandinavian redwood and teak, with emphasis on durable detailing. The market also includes compost bins and tool stores. The conclusion identifies this as a growth niche for woodworking professionals.
