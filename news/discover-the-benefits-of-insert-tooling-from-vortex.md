## Source summary

Vortex Tool Co. will showcase its Laser Edge Diamond PCD insert tooling at IWF 2026. The tooling features fully replaceable CNC PCD profile inserts that eliminate the need for re-tipping or service. Laser-cut diamond edges extend tool life, and the geometry is optimized for smooth chip flow and cut quality. Insert changes are easy and fast, reducing downtime and labor. Many profiles are compatible with select Vortex insert tool bodies. Key benefits include extended tool life, simplified maintenance, enhanced finish quality, and reduced tooling costs.
