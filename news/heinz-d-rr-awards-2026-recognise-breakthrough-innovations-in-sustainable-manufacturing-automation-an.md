## Source summary

The Dürr Group presented Heinz Dürr Awards 2026 to five teams for innovations in sustainable manufacturing, automation, and customer experience. Winners included projects: Paint Shop of the Future, battery cell filling system, HOMAG INTELLIGENCE software platform, EcoSupply P paint supply system, and HOMAG's immersive LIGNA 2025 exhibition. Each team received €7,500. Awards were presented by Prof. Dr. Dr. Alexandra Dürr and Karoline Dürr.
