## Source summary

Rometal introduces the Coplanar C-1000, a minimalist sliding system for integrating or separating spaces. Available in manual and automatic versions, the automatic version can be activated by presence sensor, touch sensor, facial recognition, or voice command. It features simplified installation, options for one or two doors, and is delivered ready for installation. The system is compact, versatile, with a sophisticated finish.
