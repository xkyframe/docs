# FSC UK partners with Professional Woodworking Expo 2026 to strengthen industry collaboration and innovation

## Source summary

FSC UK has partnered with Professional Woodworking Expo (PWE) 2026, a trade event for the UK woodworking and joinery industry, taking place at NEC Birmingham from 23 to 25 June 2026. The event expects over 6,000 visitors and 200 exhibiting brands. FSC UK subscribers receive complimentary VIP tickets. The expo is co-located with InstallerSHOW and the Painting & Decorating Show. Exhibitors include IMA Schelling, Festool, Nederman, Doucet, and Leitz. The event features live demonstrations, expert speakers on AI in joinery, UK timber regulations, material innovation, and compliance. Networking and business growth are key objectives.
