## Source summary

The global finger-jointed lumber market is forecast to grow at a CAGR of 3.8% from 2026 to 2035, driven by demand from construction and industrial packaging. Finger-jointed lumber commands a 10–25% price premium over solid kiln-dried lumber. In North America, it accounts for 15–20% of structural lumber production. The electronics packaging segment is growing at 4–6% annually. Raw material cost volatility (30–50% year-over-year swings) and trade barriers (15–25% added costs) are key constraints. The market is fragmented with large integrated producers and specialized firms.
