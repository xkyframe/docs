## Source summary

Xylino, a five-storey residential development in Almere, Netherlands, is the country's first large-scale modular housing scheme built entirely from Laminated Veneer Lumber (LVL). Completed in early 2026 by De Alliantie Ontwikkeling B.V. and Koopmans Bouwgroep, it delivers 103 homes using 436 prefabricated timber modules manufactured by geWOONhout. Modules arrived with integrated technical systems, and installation followed a sprint-based process installing 8-12 modules daily, allowing an entire block to be assembled in four weeks. Digital twins and QR codes support maintenance and end-of-life management. Kerto LVL was selected for its high strength-to-weight ratio, using up to 50% less material than alternative mass timber products. CNC machining achieved tolerances within 0.5 mm. The structure achieves R120 fire resistance and meets acoustic requirements using olivine aggregate and decouplers. Circular design principles enable future disassembly and reuse.
