# CE & Safety Compliance for Framing Machinery

Cutting and pneumatic joining machines need guarding, emergency stop reach, and declared conformity — especially when resold through EU partners.

## Core documents to request

Ask for Declaration of Conformity, electrical schematic, risk assessment summary, and maintenance instructions in English (plus local language if required). For pneumatic underpinners, include air supply spec, pressure relief, and hand placement diagrams.

- Declaration of Conformity (DoC) with directive references
- User manual with lockout/tagout steps
- Spare parts list for guards and switches
- Training note for blade change and nail magazine service

## On-machine expectations

Blade guards on miter and stack cutters must not be bypassed in production. Joiners need two-hand or light-curtain options where hands enter the clamp zone. Verify E-stop category and reset procedure matches your site safety policy.

## US vs EU emphasis

US buyers often focus on OSHA-aligned guarding and documented LOTO; EU buyers require CE marking and notified-body involvement where applicable. XKY ships with CE-oriented documentation on flagship NC/NN/CT models — confirm scope per SKU in your quote.

## Technical FAQ

**Does CE automatically satisfy UK or US requirements?**

No. CE covers EU market rules; UKCA and US compliance may need separate assessment. Use CE docs as a baseline, not a universal certificate.

**Can we install without local electrical inspection?**

Follow your jurisdiction. Provide machine nameplate, power draw, and earthing requirements to your electrician before energizing.

**Where is compliance noted on quotes?**

Request CE/safety documentation scope in /contact/quote with machine model — application engineering confirms per configuration.
